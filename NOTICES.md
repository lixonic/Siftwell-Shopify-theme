# External services and assets

The theme's MIT license covers Siftwell's project-owned source. It does not change licenses or terms for services, content or code supplied separately.

- Shopify renders Liquid objects/filters and provides commerce, accounts, checkout, hosted fonts, media players, model-viewer/XR and gift-card QR support. Platform code requested at runtime remains Shopify's or its providers' code; it is not bundled here under MIT.
- YouTube and Vimeo media use their respective services when a merchant adds those links.
- Store logos, product photographs, videos, models, product text and customer content belong to the merchant or their licensors. Demo product media, font binaries, fixture catalogs and store exports are not included.
- README screenshots under `docs/images/` show the project's Auto demo for illustration. These presentation images are excluded from the installable theme; see their [image notes](docs/images/README.md).
- Social media and Shopify brand identifiers are used to identify their services. The MIT license does not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement.
- Development-only packages are recorded in `package-lock.json` and retain their own licenses. No `node_modules` or third-party runtime bundle is committed or packaged into the theme.

The old restricted Skeleton SEO implementation and uncertain media SVGs were replaced before this repository's initial publication. The old license is not presented as MIT; previous private checkpoints retain their original notices and remain outside this repository.
