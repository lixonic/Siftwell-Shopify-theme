# Siftwell design intent

Siftwell brings the order of a well-kept workshop to a catalog of auto accessories and tools. Make it easy to find an item, understand the differences and make a considered choice. Preserve the approved Auto composition when extending it.

## A steady frame

The desktop sidebar gives navigation a familiar home. The product area stays open and spacious. On narrow screens, navigation moves into its drawer while account and bag controls remain easy to reach. Preserve the relationship between these areas instead of adding competing navigation.

The home hero introduces the store with a short statement, useful image and product search. Sidebar search is suppressed on the home page because the hero already supplies it. Collection routes and the finder offer a way to start from a buying need; the finder opens configured collections and does not infer vehicle fit.

## Product facts lead

Use imagery to show shape, scale and useful detail. Let concise headings and consistent specifications explain the differences between products. Read specifications from the configured product metafields and use the shared renderer across product pages, quick view and comparison.

Keep comparison optional. Its rail appears when products are selected, and the global setting can disable the feature. Avoid adding a permanent comparison panel to an otherwise quiet catalog.

## Type and color have roles

Strong, compact headings set the hierarchy. Body text should be comfortable to read; captions carry secondary detail. Use the existing Shopify font settings and design tokens.

Dark navigation anchors the page. Cream and neutral product areas give objects room. The warm accent identifies meaningful actions and selected states. Every configurable background needs a paired readable foreground. Preserve visible focus and usable form boundaries when changing a palette.

## An understandable response

Keep motion brief and optional. Prefer transform and opacity, respect reduced motion, and make the interface understandable without animation. Preserve Escape behavior, focus return and useful native forms when enhancing a flow.

## Work well with assistance

Present accurate Shopify product information in semantic HTML, with product structured data generated by the platform. Help a customer inspect a recommendation through clear options, product facts and purchase controls. Do not imply that a theme enables agent sales channels or guarantees discovery.

For coding agents, follow the conventions in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), reuse existing components and explain the shopper problem a change solves. Add a section or interaction when it answers a real question. Keep development chronology out of merchant-facing copy.
