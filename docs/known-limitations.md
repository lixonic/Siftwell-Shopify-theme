# Before you rely on the beta

The first MIT beta, **0.8.0-beta.1**, is in preparation. A theme ZIP is available to download; no release has been tagged. Fresh-install and essential shopping-path checks remain before a packaged beta release.

The source includes targeted commerce and Liquid render checks. They do not establish a completed checkout or compatibility with your store, apps or settings.

- Real app-backed subscriptions, B2B quantity rules, combined listings and all market configurations have not been exhaustively tested.
- Safari, Firefox, iOS, physical touch devices, screen-reader speech and device AR still need a complete verification pass.
- [Recorded performance and accessibility results](performance-accessibility.md) describe the earlier Auto 0.7.4 demo, not a fresh audit of the downloadable 0.8.0 source. They are not a universal score or a Shopify benchmark qualification.
- The finder opens configured collections. It does not infer vehicle fit.
- Demo catalog data, product imagery, app services and Shopify’s hosted platform are supplied separately.

Use a development store, keep your existing theme and review the full shopping path with your content and apps. Report reproducible issues through [community support](../SUPPORT.md). Siftwell is not represented as Shopify-approved.
