# Start with Siftwell

An open-source Shopify theme for auto accessories and tools, built to help customers find what they need, understand the details and compare their options.

## Build around the buying decision

Siftwell suits catalogs where fit, materials and intended use help someone choose. It gives customers a few useful ways into the products:

- **Search by name.** Product search gives someone who knows the item a direct starting point.
- **Start with a need.** Collection links and the product finder can begin with a job such as Road trips or Garage projects.
- **Compare the options.** Quick view opens product details from the catalog, and optional comparison puts up to three products side by side.

Choose the collections behind each route. The finder follows your choices; accurate vehicle-fit information belongs in your product content.

## Enter useful details once

Keep Weight, Material, Fit, Feature and Best in your product metafields. Siftwell uses those same fields on the product page, in quick view and in comparison, so customers can read a consistent set of details wherever they inspect an item.

Use the same units and terms across similar products. A specific fit description or material is more useful than another promotional sentence.

## Keep the catalog in view

A steady desktop sidebar keeps product groups close. Open product areas, direct headings and restrained color give practical items room to explain themselves. On a phone, navigation moves into a drawer while account and bag controls stay in the header.

Build on that order: a few recognizable categories, a clear overview photograph and one useful thought at a time. Add a section when it answers a customer’s question.

## Make the source your own

Use Shopify’s theme editor to arrange sections, product blocks, branding, colors and fonts. Developers can also adapt the MIT-licensed source and share improvements while keeping the license notice.

For a developer using a coding agent, the source includes [architecture rules](../AGENTS.md) and [design intent](../DESIGN.md) to guide changes that preserve the storefront’s structure and behavior.

## When shopping starts with an assistant

A customer may arrive with a recommendation already in mind. Siftwell’s role is to help them inspect it: clear product facts, visible options and useful comparisons. Its pages use semantic HTML and Shopify-generated product structured data.

Shopify provides the agent commerce channels and transaction services. Availability depends on the store and service setup; installing Siftwell does not activate those channels. See [Shopify’s agent commerce guide](https://shopify.dev/docs/agents).

Explore the [Auto demo](https://siftwell-theme.myshopify.com/) with visitor password `siftwell` to see the layout and product details in context.

## Build your first shopping path

Start with a small catalog, connect one buying need to a collection, then follow a product through to the bag. The first MIT beta is in preparation; evaluate it in a development store and read the [current limitations](known-limitations.md) before planning a launch.
